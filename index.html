It's a bit like home broadband, but rather than using your own phone line, we install a central, high-capacity internet connection and use WiFi access points to send that connection all around your site. You can then get online by connecting to this network and use it like you would home broadband.

Like mobile phone coverage, the speed you get depends on how close you are to the WiFi access point or if there are thick walls in the way. Your speeds will also vary depending on things like time of day and number of other users.
